## Critical Architecture Boundary

```text
Task or word problem
  → proposed interpretation (fallible)
  → reviewed and approved interpretation
  → deterministic topic model
  → verified consequences and learner actions
  → age-appropriate teaching plan and interactive representation
```

Mathematical consistency does not establish semantic fidelity to a story. LLM agreement is not proof. Teaching suitability needs its own review. Geometry, exploration, classification, and construction tasks must work without mandatory word-problem or equation fields.
