## Proposed objective baseline

- **Comparison:** retain the three additive unknown positions. P2 placement has provider evidence; P3 historical assessment now corroborates the task family, but does not close the second P2-specific check. Prerequisites: addition/subtraction, comparing quantities, and reading one relationship.
- **Geometry:** propose P3 right-angle recognition and comparison with a right angle. Three task types: identify a right angle; classify an angle relative to a right angle; select all right angles from differently oriented examples. Prerequisites: recognise a corner/turn and compare with a supplied right-angle reference. Avoid requiring degree measurement or the terms acute/obtuse in the pilot.
- **Source disagreement:** IXL's P3 page uses a 2006 alignment; Practicle presents its page as current 2026 coverage. Agreement supports the narrow candidate, not a claim that the complete syllabuses match.
- **M0 status:** evidence preparation advanced; human review owner remains unassigned. Two-source review and baseline acceptance are not complete. Codex is the preparer, not the independent human approver.
